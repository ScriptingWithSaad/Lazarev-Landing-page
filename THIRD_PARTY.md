# Third-party assets

This educational landing-page recreation retains the Lazarev branding, illustrations, logos, project videos and design content already present in the repository. It is not the official agency website. Original media files remain in `assets/images` and `assets/videos`; generated previews live in `assets/optimized`.

- GSAP and ScrollTrigger 3.12.5, distributed from the official npm package. Their copyright and license notices remain in the vendor files. See <https://gsap.com/standard-license/>.
- Lenis 1.3.26, MIT license, distributed from its official npm package. The license is in `assets/vendor/lenis-LICENSE.txt`. Source: <https://github.com/darkroomengineering/lenis>.
- Manrope variable font, SIL Open Font License 1.1. The license is in `assets/fonts/manrope-LICENSE.txt`. Source: <https://github.com/sharanda/manrope>.

All runtime libraries and the font are served locally; the page does not depend on a third-party CDN to render or respond to input.
